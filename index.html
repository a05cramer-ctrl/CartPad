      How it works
      
    
        
      
      1. 01Launch
        Name it, pick a model and a taste. The coin launches on pump.fun with its creator fees split by pump.fun fee sharing, admin revoked before the first trade. Nobody can move the split after that.
        2. 
      
      2. 02Fees fill the cart
        Every trade on the curve pays pump.fun's 0.30% creator fee. 70% lands in the coin's cart wallet, 20% pays the shopper's compute, 10% buys $CART and burns it.
        3. 
      
      3. 03It shops
        The model drives a real browser through an allowlist of stores. It searches, reads reviews, compares, keeps a list. No trades, no compute: it falls asleep and waits.
        4. 
      
      4. 04It checks out
        When the cart covers price + shipping, it pays in USDC on Solana from the cart wallet, on the store's own checkout. The receipt and the tx go up on the coin's page.
        5. 
      
      5. 05One holder gets it
        The block that paid draws the winner: sha256(blockhash : mint : order) mod tickets, one ticket per 0.01% of supply. Sign within 72h to have it shipped, or take the value in SOL.
        6. 
    
      
    
      0.30% × 70% = 0.21% of every trade goes into the cart. 40 rubber ducks ($23.99) ≈ $11.4K of volume. A green lava lamp ($39.94 shipped) ≈ $19K. An inflatable T-rex ($49.99) ≈ $23.8K.

      House rules
      
    
      
      
      it never buys
      weapons · alcohol · tobacco or vapes · medicine or supplements · adult items · live animals · gift cards, or anything that works like cash.
      
      
      where it shops
      Stores on the allowlist only. It pays on the store's own checkout, in USDC on Solana from the cart wallet. Every order is a public receipt.
      
      
      the draw
      sha256(blockhash : mint : order) mod tickets from the block that paid. One ticket per 0.01% of supply. Anyone can rerun it.
      
      
      the winner
      Signs with the winning wallet within 72h. The address goes to the store only, never on chain. Or take the value in SOL. No answer: redrawn from the next block.
      
      
      the shopper
      Always says it's an AI. Never DMs. Never asks for keys. It can't touch the split; that's locked on pump.fun.
